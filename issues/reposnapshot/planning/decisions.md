# Decisions

Running registry of **decisions and their reasons** — what was settled, why, and
what it would cost to reverse. Completed work lives in [ledger.md](ledger.md) and
what remains in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md); `payload-manifest-ledger.md` is a
separate registry tracking declarations the *payload* owes a reader, where this
one tracks calls the *project* has made.

Filed, not argued. Full rationale lives in the linked docs; this is the index
that stops a settled question being reopened from scratch, and stops a
deliberate exception being "fixed" by someone who reads it as an oversight.

Status vocabulary: `settled` · `leaning` (direction stated, not final) · `open` ·
`superseded`.

## Wire rendering (2026-08-22)

| # | decision | why it is consequential | status | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 49 | **A row is a list of ITEMS joined by exactly one space.** Items are values, keys (a key carries its trailing colon), and the structural marks `\|` `[` `]` `,` — every mark is its own item, so no mark abuts a value. A sub-grammar with its own internal syntax (a type expression `int(4)`, the encoded content span) renders itself FIRST and enters as **one** item; its internal spacing is its own business. Header: `gidx: int(4) \| path: string \| content_meta: [ line_mean: double , num_chars: int ] \| content_bytes: int \| content: string`. Record: `0003 \| TODO.md \| [ 103.4146 , 4281 ] \| 4328 \| <span>`. **There is no padding property and must not be one** — padding is what the join does | **It is a simplification, not a decoration.** Deletes the framing quartet (`FieldDelimiter` · `BlockOpen` · `BlockClose` · `BlockDelimiter`) in favour of one join rule, and makes the writer's arithmetic exact by construction: **row bytes = Σ item bytes + (item count − 1) + record_delimiter**, with nothing hidden inside a concatenation (today the block's internal delimiters are buried in one pre-assembled piece). Templates become item *arrays* and stop carrying spacing, so the declaration states the rule once instead of per mark — the property that survives a language port (#2). **The token argument is noise and was not the reason**: measured on a real 4.6 MB payload the spacing costs **+0.046%** against fully-tight, and buys back the one genuine boundary artifact — under tight `\|` the token `]\|` merged the block close with the column separator in **102/102** rows; padded, zero straddles at any depth. **Lexing stays structural** (split on `" \| "`, then `" , "`), never a bare whitespace split: items may contain spaces — content does, and `path` did in **23.5%** of a real corpus (chat-export fixtures). Reinforces #45: with marks as items there is no trailing-mark item to drop | settled 2026-08-22 | `container.spec.jsonc` v0.4 §`shard_container_schema.item_join` + §invariants; this session |

## Shard packing (2026-08-16)

| # | decision | why it is consequential | status | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40 | **`Greedy \| Balanced \| Loose` retired.** Packing is governed by `ShardQuotaBytes` + `ShardToleranceBytes` (exact bytes, no percentages) + `OrderStrict` + `PackObjective`; objective per group is lexicographic — fewest shards under the ceiling, then the `PackObjective` shape (see #48; was fixed at least total overshoot) | Objective is tightest packing, not evenness; the ×1.1/×0.8 constants had no derivation. Clause 2 is what keeps quota from being decorative in flexible mode. Renamed from `MaxShardSizeBytes` / LTS "Span": it is a Size (whole written file), and Span is content-measure vocabulary. **No preset/profile layer over the knobs** (2026-08-18) — the LTS profiles were never liked; quota + tolerance semantics are the whole surface, and a name over a tuple is how three names became one number | settled 2026-08-16; no-presets 2026-08-18 | shards-brief §Objective, §Knob roster; discussion/opus-export-packing-discussion |
| 41 | **Tolerance is a feasibility bound for shard-eliminating moves, not routine fill; quota is the objective.** Records are enumerated and measured *before* packing, which turns packing into a pure integer problem | The "tolerance ≡ bigger quota" equivalence holds only for a sequential-admit packer; once the packer optimizes over a known size vector, the two numbers do different jobs. Enumerate-first is what makes that possible and makes the packer testable on synthetic vectors | settled 2026-08-16 | shards-brief §Policy stack, §Algorithm |
| 42 | **Overflow is policy:** `headerBytes + rowBytes > ceiling` → own shard, header + one row, any size; declared to the reader; excluded from total/max overshoot and LB comparisons. `AllowOversizedShards` switch retired (leaning) | Atomicity outranks quota; the reader's safety statement is "every shard ≤ ceiling except those flagged oversized". Counting oversized excess as overshoot would let one file swamp the packer's number. **Singleton ≠ InBand ≠ Oversized ≠ runt** — four classifications, exact bytes, kept apart in diagnostics; empty group ≠ singleton group | settled 2026-08-16 | shards-brief §Policy stack |
| 43 | **Order is a per-group knob** (`GroupSort`, default `RelativePath` asc); flexible membership is legitimate under any `Grouping`; group containment is structural (no cross-group stage); each group carries its own tail by design | Directory-listing adjacency is a human convention; the reading agent seeks by tree offsets and gains nothing from it. Per-group tails are a property of grouping, not waste to optimize away. `Get-PathHash` survives only as an optional sort key — FFD does the dispersion job | settled 2026-08-16 | shards-brief §Policy stack; memory reposnapshot-developer-frame |
| 44 | **The packer is deterministic; no stochastic search.** Strict: greedy contiguous (optimal at quota); strict + tolerance: `k_min` = greedy-at-ceiling, then the shape — `FrontLoad`: greedy-at-quota + adjacent merge; `Even`: linear partition (both exact; #48); flexible: FFD then tolerance-bounded bin elimination, stop at `Gap`. Repair pass = **roadmap item pending v1 MVP results** | A GA over permutations is a complete encoding but needs a recorded seed and is n! wide for a problem FFD solves within 11/9·OPT; determinism is an exit gate. Runts always land as group tails via a threshold-free step-7 rule (min-fill bin last) | settled 2026-08-16; strict+tolerance procedure settled 2026-08-18 via #48 | shards-brief §Algorithm |
| 45 | **Row termination is LF alone; the trailing `\|` is dropped.** UTF-8 without BOM, `\n` only; the writer controls newline bytes explicitly | The container is newline-delimited, one physical line per row (jsonl-like); the terminator is redundant and confusing. Byte-exact planning depends on these encoding invariants being stated, not assumed — a BOM or CRLF shifts every offset | **settled 2026-08-22** — propagated to shard-container-brief; #49 makes it structural (no trailing-mark item exists) and `container.tests` asserts LF-only + no trailing mark | shards-brief §Planning is exact; shard-container-brief §Wire order and roles |
| 46 | **Vocabulary:** *run* = one execution/payload; *sidx* = fixed-width ordinal in the shard **filename**, not a row column; *idx* = optional global address, one scheme among several — which address columns v3 carries is **open**. Rule: any position-dependent row field is fixed-width from a bound known before packing | "run" was being overloaded for a group's shard sequence; sidx was being imagined as a row field. Fixed-width is what lets values be assigned after packing while row bytes stay exact (LTS's plain-integer idx is the counterexample). Header schema is the superset; the row is its shadow — one source for measure and render | settled 2026-08-16 (address columns: open) | shards-brief §Vocabulary; shard-container-brief (owed) |
| 47 | **No fragmentation on the v3 critical path.** A record is one ingested file, pre-assembled and measured whole. AST-based fragmentation (e.g. markdown → one record per H2, semantic sub-addressing) is a future, non-blocking roadmap item | Packing optimizes over fixed whole rows; fragmentation would make record boundaries a degree of freedom — a different problem. The packer contract is already fragmentation-agnostic, so the future work lands upstream without touching this stage | settled 2026-08-16 | shards-brief §Roadmap; memory reposnapshot-developer-frame |
| 48 | **Clause 2 of the objective is a *shape*, exposed as `PackObjective = FrontLoad \| Even`, both implemented; "least overshoot" is clause 3, an invariant of both shapes, not a value. Tolerance is a per-shard bound (wiggle room for filling an underutilized shard), overshoot the per-shard measurement — a bound is not consumed, so no "spent" vocabulary. Aggregates follow the split: `MaxOvershootBytes` is the tolerance-facing check (`≤ ShardToleranceBytes`), `TotalOvershootBytes` the overshoot-facing diagnostic (clause 3, harness). Per shard the primitive is signed `DeviationBytes = PlannedSizeBytes − quota` — anchored at quota, so `< 0` slack · `0…tolerance` overshoot · `> tolerance` oversized; overshoot, slack and `Class` derive from it, and the clamp `max(0,·)` is never the stored fact.** `FrontLoad` (working default): fill to quota in nominal order, remainder as the group's tail — `k−1` full shards + one tail. `Even`: spread the remainder — minimize the largest shard, then the next (lexicographic min-max, in optimization terms). Clause 1 (fewest shards) is unchanged and first, so `ShardCount` is identical under both. **Overflow outranks all of it**: an oversized record (row alone > ceiling) gets its own shard at any size — atomicity is the anti-fragmentation guarantee; the objectives govern only the packable set. Comparison harness in the battery, not the shipping path | **Why a shape knob, and why these two.** `k` is always the minimum; what varies is where the sub-shard remainder lands (`Σ_g` is almost never an exact multiple of capacity; the slack is < one shard). Concentrate it (full shards + tail — what "pack as tightly as possible" names) or spread it (no shard far from its siblings) — different readers, different answers, and only running both on real payloads says which is the default. **Why not `MinOvershoot` as a value** (the 08-18 morning draft): Σ `max(0, size−quota)` at fixed `k` and `Σ_g` is Schur-convex — the flattest arrangement always minimizes it — so an "even" objective is a *refinement* of "min overshoot", not an alternative; and among zero-overshoot arrangements min-overshoot is indifferent, leaving the shape to the tie-break. Neither of those is front-loading. So min-overshoot is a constraint both shapes honor, and the real second axis is `FrontLoad` vs `Even`. **The 08-16 "strict + tolerance" open call closes by this**: its two candidate procedures (greedy-at-quota + adjacent merge; greedy-at-ceiling + DP) were the two shapes, not two ways to reach one. Exactness inverts the usual expectation — contiguous is exact for both shapes, flexible is LPT/FFD-approximate — so `Even` is more even under `OrderStrict`, making strict a real choice rather than a concession | settled 2026-08-18 (default: open) | shards-brief §Objective, §Knob roster, §Algorithm 5, §Exit gate; discussion/opus-export-packing-discussion |

## Notes

- **Two traps have now bitten twice each in this codebase**, and a third occurrence
  argues for `AGENTS.md` rather than a per-file comment:
  - *If-expression single-element unroll* — an if-expression enumerates its
    output, collapsing a one-element array to a scalar. Hit in
    `rs.core.assemble.psm1` (Phase 5) and again in `format-ws.ps1`'s receipt.
  - *Member access on an empty array writes to the error stream* — and colonel
    clears `$Error` before each processor call and attributes what it finds per
    item, so the common case hangs a spurious error on **every** item. Same
    mechanism as the `Set-StrictMode`-in-processor-bodies finding.
- **`run-all.ps1`'s error-stream check earns its keep.** It caught the empty-array
  defect while 764 asserts passed. A green battery is two signals, not one —
  count *and* clean stream.
- Where an entry is `open` or `leaning`, the live discussion lives in its source
  doc; this ledger records that the call is owed, not what it should be.
