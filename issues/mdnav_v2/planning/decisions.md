| D41 | *(amends D11, D13; canon for the structural model is [design/mdnav_v2_structure-brief.md](../design/mdnav_v2_structure-brief.md))* **One recursive procedure discovers both hierarchies; the result is flat rows with structure in a path.** Per window: carve regions (opaque leaves + containers, state-driven; parity for residue only) → mask → partition the mask by headings → recurse down the spine (level+1) and into enterable containers (switched context, source coordinates) → collect inline leaves over the mask. Coverage stays byte-based. The reader's surface is a telescope — `tree`/`read`/`find`/`status`, each `tree` row carrying a census of what is under it — never chunks; framing wraps results. Non-goal sharpened: no *inline* parser; block structure by shape and block conditions is the model.
